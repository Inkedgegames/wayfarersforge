需要幫忙嗎?

遇到問題、發現錯誤,或只是想說說話,都歡迎寫信到 inkedgegames@gmail.com。來信時附上裝置型號和遊戲版本(在「設定」裡),會更快找到問題。

常見問題

免費試玩可以玩到哪裡?

可以玩到第二章的中段。之後可以一次買斷完整版,一路玩到結局,沒有廣告,也不用登入。

買過完整版,換手機或重裝後怎麼辦?

到遊戲的「設定 → 🌟 完整版」,按「恢復購買」。請用當初購買的同一個 Apple ID。

按了購買卻沒有解鎖?

請確認網路連線,然後到「設定 → 🌟 完整版」按「恢復購買」。如果購買需要家長核准,核准後就會自動解鎖。

存檔放在哪裡?

存檔只在你的裝置上。刪除遊戲會一起刪掉存檔,請小心。

怎麼換語言?

在「設定」裡可以切換中文和英文。

Need a hand?

Found a bug, got stuck, or just want to say hi? Email inkedgegames@gmail.com. Including your device model and the game version (shown in Settings) helps us find the problem faster.

FAQ

How far does the free trial go?

To the middle of Chapter 2. After that, a one-time purchase unlocks the full game, all the way to the ending. No ads, no login.

I bought the full game. What about a new phone or a reinstall?

Open Settings → 🌟 Full Game in the game and tap Restore Purchases, using the same Apple ID you bought it with.

I paid but the game didn't unlock.

Check your internet connection, then tap Restore Purchases in Settings → 🌟 Full Game. If the purchase needs a parent's approval, it unlocks as soon as it's approved.

Where are my saves?

Only on your device. Deleting the game deletes its saves too, so be careful.

How do I change the language?

You can switch between Chinese and English in Settings.

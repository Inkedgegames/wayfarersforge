隱私權政策

生效日期:2026 年 11 月 5 日

《時光匠師》是一款單機遊戲。我們不收集、不儲存、也不分享你的任何個人資料。

我們不收集的東西

  • 不需要註冊或登入,沒有帳號。
  • 沒有廣告,也沒有任何廣告追蹤。
  • 沒有數據分析、沒有使用統計、不會回傳當機報告。
  • 不會讀取你的聯絡人、照片、位置或其他裝置資料。

存檔

你的遊戲進度只存在你自己的裝置上,不會上傳到我們這裡。刪除遊戲時,存檔也會一起刪除。

購買(iOS)

解鎖完整版是透過 Apple 的 App Store 付款,由 Apple 處理。我們拿不到你的姓名、付款資料或 Apple ID,只會知道「這台裝置已經解鎖」。相關資料請參考 Apple 的隱私權政策。

Steam(電腦版)

在 Steam 上遊玩時,解鎖的成就會傳給 Steam,適用 Valve 的隱私權政策。

兒童

我們不收集任何人的資料,包括兒童。

政策變更

如果以後遊戲加入會用到資料的功能,我們會先更新這一頁,並修改上面的生效日期。

聯絡我們

有任何問題,請寫信到 inkedgegames@gmail.com。

Privacy Policy

Effective: November 5, 2026

The Wayfarer's Forge is a single-player game. We don't collect, store or share any of your personal data.

What we don't collect

  • No sign-up or login. There are no accounts.
  • No ads and no ad tracking.
  • No analytics, no usage stats, no crash reports sent anywhere.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location or other device data.

Your saves

Your progress is saved only on your own device and is never uploaded to us. If you delete the game, its saves are deleted too.

Purchases (iOS)

Unlocking the full game is paid through Apple's App Store and handled by Apple. We never see your name, payment details or Apple ID, only that the game has been unlocked. See Apple's privacy policy for details.

Steam (PC)

When you play on Steam, achievements you unlock are sent to Steam under Valve's privacy policy.

Children

We collect no data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If the game ever adds a feature that uses data, we'll update this page first and change the date above.
